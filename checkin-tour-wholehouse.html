Hot Tub

The Hot Tub

Wood-fired — plan ahead

🕐
Allow 3–4 Hours to HeatLight it 3 to 4 hours before you want to get in.
1
Remove the lid from the small metal bucket.
2
Place wood inside the metal bucket.
3
Light the fire and replace the lid.
4
Stir the water every few minutes when adding more wood — this speeds up heating significantly.
5
Wait 3–4 hours — then enjoy!
