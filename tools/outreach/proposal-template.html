ID verification

🔄 Booking process & payments

How a booking — and its payout — actually flows.

📩 Booking flow
1
Traveller requests a booking for a specific date and time window.
2
You accept or decline the request from your Host Dashboard.
3
Traveller arrives and shows their PIN — you check bags in as active.
4
Traveller returns and shows the same PIN — you check bags out as complete.
💳 Payment flow
✓
Travellers pay upfront when they book, through Cubby's payment processor.
✓
Cubby holds the funds until the booking is marked complete.
✓
On completion, the total booking value is split 70/30 — you receive 70%, Cubby retains 30% (covering payment processing, verification, and support).
✓
Payouts are currently sent manually by the Cubby team, by EFT, to the bank details you provide.
⚠ To be confirmed

Exact payout turnaround time (e.g. same-week vs. monthly) is not yet fixed — we'll confirm this before public launch. Founding Hosts will hear about it first.

❓ Frequently asked questions

The questions we'd ask, in your position.

Do I have to accept every booking request?
No — you review each request and choose to accept or decline it from your Host Dashboard.
Can I block out dates or pause my listing?
Yes — you control your own hours and days, and can pause your listing at any time.
Do I need to be available every day?
No — Cubby only shows your listing as bookable during the times you choose.
How long can luggage stay with me?
Bookings are same-day — a set drop-off and pick-up window on the date the traveller books, not multi-day storage.
What happens if a traveller arrives late? TBC
To be confirmed before public launch — an exact grace-period or late-collection process isn't finalised yet.
What if I need to cancel a booking I've accepted? TBC
To be confirmed before public launch. In the meantime, contact the traveller and the Cubby team directly.
How do disputes get handled? TBC
To be confirmed before public launch — Cubby's admin team is always the point of contact if a booking doesn't go as expected.
What if a bag is lost or damaged in my care? TBC
No confirmed insurance or liability process exists yet — this will be explained clearly before public launch.
How quickly do I get paid?
You receive 70% of the total booking value once complete, paid manually by EFT — exact turnaround is to be confirmed before public launch.
How much time does this actually take, day to day?
Mostly at drop-off and collection — checking a PIN and storing or returning a bag takes a few minutes. No ongoing effort beyond that.
Is there a contract or minimum commitment?
No — listing on Cubby is free, with no lock-in. You can pause or leave at any time.
Can I offer Bag Runner (delivery) services too?
Not at this stage — Bag Runners is a planned future service and isn't part of the initial Cape Town launch.
