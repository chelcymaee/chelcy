Founding Host Proposal

Prepared for Business name

Cubby is a Cape Town based luggage storage platform that connects travellers with trusted local businesses where they can safely store their luggage for a few hours. We help travellers who arrive before check-in, have already checked out of their accommodation, or simply want to enjoy their time in Cape Town without carrying their luggage around.

How hosting works

  • You choose the hours you're able to accept bookings — no fixed schedule required.
  • Travellers book and pay in advance through the Cubby app; you simply confirm the request.
  • You store the luggage securely, then hand it back when the traveller collects it.
  • Cubby handles the booking, payment and customer support — you focus on your own business as usual.

Why become a Founding Host

  • An additional income stream from space you already have, during hours you choose.
  • More visitors discovering your business as they come and go to store or collect luggage.
  • Bookings are verified and prepaid, so there's no cash handling or chasing payment.
  • Founding Hosts help shape how Cubby works in Cape Town from the very start.

Is this a fit?

If you have a suitable secure area and staffed hours when you could accept a booking, there's a good chance it's worth a conversation — no pressure, and no commitment to see the full details.
