⚙️ How it works

What a booking looks like, from your side.

1

Traveller finds you

Your listing appears to nearby travellers searching Cubby, with your hours, pricing and photos.

2

Booking request comes in

You see the date, time window and number of bags in your Host Dashboard.

3

Bags dropped off

The traveller shows their unique PIN. You check it, store the bags, mark the booking active.

4

Bags collected

Traveller returns, shows the same PIN, you hand the bags back and mark it complete.

❓ Questions

Frequently asked.

Do I have to accept every booking request?

No — you review and choose to accept or decline each one from your Host Dashboard.

How long can luggage stay with me?

Bookings are same-day — a set drop-off and pick-up window, not multi-day storage.

Is there a contract or minimum commitment?

No — listing is free, with no lock-in. Pause or leave at any time.

How quickly do I get paid?

70% of the total booking value once complete, paid manually by EFT. Exact turnaround TBC before public launch.

What if a bag is lost or damaged in my care?

No confirmed insurance or liability process exists yet TBC — this will be explained clearly before public launch.

🙋 Who can become a host

If you have secure space, there's a good chance you qualify.

What you'll need: a secure, lockable space to store bags for the day; valid ID for verification; South African bank account details for payouts; and consistent availability during the hours you set.

Onboarding is currently team-assisted rather than fully self-serve — after your application is approved, the Cubby team sets up your listing together with you.
