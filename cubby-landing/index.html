❓ Good questions

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers, no fine print surprises.

For travellers
How does Cubby work?

Find a nearby host — a café, hotel or guesthouse — on the map, book a drop-off window, and bring your bags over. You'll get a unique PIN to check your bags in and collect them later — no cash or paperwork needed.

How are hosts verified?

Every host completes ID verification before their space can go live on Cubby, and our team reviews each application before it's approved.

What happens if something goes wrong with my bags?

We vet hosts before they join, and every booking is tracked with a secure PIN and a timestamped check-in and check-out. If anything feels off during your booking, our support team is on hand to help you sort it out directly with your host. Cubby doesn't currently offer a blanket insurance guarantee, so we'd recommend keeping valuables with you and relying on your own travel insurance for high-value items.

Is my payment and information secure?

Bookings will be processed through a secure payment provider, and hosts never see your card details — only what they need to confirm your drop-off.

Are Bag Runners or airport collection available yet?

Not yet — those are on our roadmap. Right now Cubby is focused on getting local bag storage right in Cape Town first, before we expand into delivery and collection.

For hosts
How do I become a Cubby host?

Apply using the form below with your business details. Our team reviews applications and verifies your identity before your listing goes live.

How do payouts work?

You set your own rate per bag. Cubby takes a small service fee from each booking to cover platform costs and pays out the rest. Exact payout timing and fee will be confirmed as we finalise our payment provider ahead of launch.

What does it cost to join?

Nothing upfront — there's no signup or listing fee. You only pay a service fee on bookings you actually complete.

Launch
When and where is Cubby launching?

We're launching in Cape Town first. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the moment bookings open in your area, with more South African cities to follow.
