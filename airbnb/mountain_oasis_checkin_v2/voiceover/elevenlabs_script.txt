Mountain Oasis: self check-in voiceover script v2 (for ElevenLabs)
One paragraph per line; leave a short pause between lines. The long pause for the stairs walk (after the 'Alternatively...' line) is added in the edit.

Welcome to Mountain Oasis!

Here's a quick guide to make your self check-in as easy as possible.

When you arrive, park in front of the property, where you'll see the blue Mountain Oasis parking signs.

Head towards the large gate, where you'll find the lockboxes.

Your keys are in the top lockbox.

Simply enter the code your host will share with you before check-in,

then press both side buttons together to open it.

Your keys come with a remote. Press the big red button to open the main gate,

and use the green key to open the elevator gate.

To reach your unit, you have two options.

If you're using the elevator to the left of the gate, press the yellow button to go up to your pathway.

To return to the parking area, press the green button.

Alternatively, enter through the large gate, and follow the signs marked Upstairs.

Welcome home! Here are a few things to know.

In the front entrance, you'll find the firewood for the hot tub and the fireplace.

We provide two bags at check-in. After that, each extra bag is one hundred rand.

The clothing rack is under the entrance table.

Your Wi-Fi details are in the welcome book on the dining room table.

Whenever you head outside, please place the pillow in front of the glass door, so it doesn't slam shut in the wind.

The left-side balcony lights are switched on here, in the living room.

For the fireplace, everything you need is in the buckets beside it.

The right-side balcony lights are switched on in the master bedroom,

and the hairdryer and iron are in the main bedroom cupboards.

That's it!

Settle in, make yourself at home, and enjoy your stay at Mountain Oasis!
