Mountain Oasis downstairs hot tub guide: voiceover script (for ElevenLabs)
One paragraph per line; leave a short pause between lines. Read the Safety & Liability part slowly and calmly (it plays over the scrolling end credits).

Welcome to the Mountain Oasis hot tub!

Here's how to fire it up, and enjoy it safely.

Fill the hot tub with water to just above the top inlet.

Place the blue geo-bubble blanket on the water surface. You'll find it inside the chalet.

Do not make a fire in the coils unless the water is at this level.

Make a fire in the coil unit, using good, dry wood, leaned up against the coils.

Do not make a large bonfire, as it could cause heat damage to the fibreglass.

Stir the water with your hand every fifteen to twenty minutes while it heats, to help circulate the heat.

It should take about one and a half to two hours to reach a bathing temperature of thirty-six to forty degrees.

Once it's there, keep it warm with a handful of charcoal or firewood every twenty minutes.

A few important notes.

If it overheats, carefully splash some water on the fire, taking care not to get burnt by the steam, or add cold water from the hosepipe straight away.

If you hear bubbling or steam noises, the coils may not be level, and the supporting leg may need adjusting.

Do not use the hot tub in windy conditions, to prevent bush fires.

Add a little water to the drip tray before use, to help stop embers from causing a fire.

And always put out the fire before leaving the hot tub unattended.

Safety and liability.

Please follow water and fire safety precautions at all times, including adult supervision of minors.

Tickle Tub and Mountain Oasis will not be held liable for any accidents involving the use of this hot tub.

Enjoy your soak!
