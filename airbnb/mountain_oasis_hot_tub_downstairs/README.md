# Mountain Oasis: downstairs hot tub guide (Tickle Tub)

1:36 vertical (1080×1920), same style as the upstairs hot tub guide. It follows the downstairs instructions only. Clips: IMG_1237.2, 1238, 1239, plus the downstairs sunsets IMG_0055–0061.

## Running order
1. **Opening:** "Mountain Oasis · hot tub guide · downstairs" over the window sunset.
2. **Before you begin:** fill to just above the top inlet (the walk to the tub). The blue geo-bubble blanket, kept in the chalet (cover coming off to reveal the blanket). No fire unless the water is at this level.
3. **Light the fire:** good dry wood leaned on the coils (opening the coil unit). No large bonfire, to protect the fibreglass.
4. **Stir and heat:** stir by hand every 15–20 minutes. It takes 1½–2 hours to reach 36–40°C. Top up with charcoal or wood every 20 minutes.
5. **Important notes** (over downstairs sunsets): overheating (splash water or add cold water from the hosepipe). Bubbling or steam noises (check the coils are level and the leg). No use in windy weather. A little water in the drip tray. Put the fire out before leaving.
6. **Safety & Liability:** film-style scrolling credits over the balcony-door and horizon sunsets, read by the voiceover.
7. **Ending:** "enjoy your soak at Mountain Oasis".
