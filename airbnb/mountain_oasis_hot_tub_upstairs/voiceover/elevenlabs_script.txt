Mountain Oasis upstairs hot tub guide: voiceover script (for ElevenLabs)
One paragraph per line; leave a short pause between lines. Read the Safety & Liability part slowly and calmly (it plays over the scrolling end credits).

Welcome to the Mountain Oasis hot tub!

Here's how to fire it up, and enjoy it safely.

First, grab some firewood from the front entrance.

Two bags are provided at check-in. After that, each extra bag is one hundred rand.

Your wood-fired hot tub is in the garden, beside the pool.

Before you begin, check the water level. It must be at the red line before you light the fire.

Never heat the hot tub if the water is below this line.

Remove the hot tub cover, and place it safely to one side.

To light the fire, place a fire starter and a few small pieces of dry wood into the wood-burning heater.

Once the fire is established, add a few more pieces of wood, and close the firebox lid.

Only touch the wooden handle on the lid. All metal surfaces become extremely hot.

Stir the water every fifteen minutes, using the large wooden paddle.

Each time you add more wood, stir the water again. This circulates the hot water, and speeds up the heating.

The hot tub generally takes two to three hours to reach a comfortable temperature, depending on how often you add wood and stir.

A few important notes.

If you need any assistance, please contact the House Manager.

Please let the House Manager know if the hot tub needs to be emptied or refilled.

Never heat the hot tub if the water level is below the marked line.

Never leave the fire unattended.

All metal surfaces become extremely hot during use. Please be careful.

Always replace the hot tub cover once the fire is out, and the hot tub is no longer in use.

Safety and liability.

Use of the hot tub is entirely at your own risk.

Mountain Oasis, its owners, and management accept no responsibility or liability for any injury, accident, loss, damage, or illness arising from the use of the hot tub.

Children must be supervised by an adult at all times while using, or near, the hot tub.

Guests are responsible for using the hot tub safely, and in accordance with these instructions.

Enjoy your soak!
