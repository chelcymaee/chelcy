5. **Stir and heat:** stir every 15 minutes with the paddle (ring follows the paddle), stir again each time you add wood. It takes 2–3 hours to heat.
6. **Important notes:** House Manager for help, emptying or refilling. Never heat below the line. Never leave the fire unattended. Metal gets hot. Cover back on when done.
7. **Safety & Liability:** film-style end credits. The disclaimer scrolls slowly up the screen in Cormorant serif over slowed upstairs sunsets, timed to the voiceover reading each line.
