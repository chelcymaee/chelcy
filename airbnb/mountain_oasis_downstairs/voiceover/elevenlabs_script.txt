Mountain Oasis downstairs unit: self check-in voiceover script (for ElevenLabs)
One paragraph per line; leave a short pause between lines. The long pause for the stairs walk (after the 'Alternatively...' line) is added in the edit.

Welcome to Mountain Oasis!

Here's a quick guide to make your self check-in to the downstairs unit as easy as possible.

When you arrive, park in front of the property, where you'll see the blue Mountain Oasis parking signs.

To reach your unit, you have two options: the elevator, or the stairs. But first, let's get your keys.

Head towards the large gate, where you'll find the lockboxes.

Your keys are in the bottom lockbox.

Simply enter the code your host will share with you before check-in,

then press both side buttons together to open it.

Your keys come with a remote. Press the big red button to open the main gate,

and use the green key to open the elevator gate.

If you're using the elevator, press the blue button. It takes you directly to your front entrance.

To return to the parking area, press the green button.

Alternatively, enter through the large gate, and follow the signs down the stairs.

At this sign, head left, towards Downstairs and Bungalow.

Use the blue key to open the small white gate.

Welcome home! The red key opens your front sliding door.

Here are a few things to know once you're inside.

Your Wi-Fi password is in the welcome book on the table.

All the keys are stored in the basket in the kitchen.

The left-side balcony lights are switched on in the living room.

The right-side balcony lights are switched on in the single bedroom.

Both balcony lights have a fifteen-minute motion sensor, and switch off automatically when no movement is detected, to save electricity.

The hairdryer and iron are in the bedroom cupboards.

That's it!

Settle in, make yourself at home, and enjoy your stay at Mountain Oasis!
