# Mountain Oasis: downstairs unit self check-in guide

1:46 vertical (1080×1920), same style as the upstairs guide (`../mountain_oasis_checkin_v2/`). It uses the downstairs clips (IMG_1232–1263, sunsets IMG_0055–0061) from the `airbnb-checkin-1` release, plus the upstairs parking, big-gate, green-key and lift-panel shots.

## Running order
1. **Sunset opening:** the title over the sunset through the downstairs window, then the 4-step card.
2. **Park out front.** The two options (elevator or stairs) are introduced, then "first, grab your keys".
3. **Grab your keys:**
   - The **bottom** lockbox (ring), entering the code, pressing both sides.
   - The remote's red button opens the main gate.
   - The green key opens the elevator gate.
4. **Option A, the elevator:** the welcome sign, then the **blue button** that goes straight to your front entrance (ring), the lift ride down, and the green button back to the parking.
   **Option B, the stairs:** a speed ramp that slows at each sign (Follow the stairs, Leg day?, Take a moment, Halfway there), each card beside its real sign. Then "Where are you heading?": head left to Downstairs + Bungalow. The **blue key** opens the small white gate.
5. **Settle in:**
   - The **red key** opens the front sliding door.
   - Wi-Fi password in the welcome book on the table.
   - All keys are stored in the kitchen basket.
   - Left balcony lights: living-room switch.
   - Right balcony lights: single bedroom.
   - 15-minute motion sensor on both.
   - Hairdryer and iron in the bedroom cupboards.
6. **Ending:** the master bedroom and the hot-tub lawn, then balcony sunsets and "enjoy your stay".

No close-up of the balcony light switches was filmed, so those lines name the rooms instead of showing a ring.
