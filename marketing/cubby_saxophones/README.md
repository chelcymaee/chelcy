# Cubby: "POV: you're going on holiday to Cape Town but the saxophones keep getting louder"

A 36 s vertical TikTok/Reel trend edit.
- **0–24 s, the chaos:** 12 shots, each used once. Mostly the new airport and airbridge clips (IMG_1547–1549, 1551, 1552, 1571–1573; IMG_1547 has no emoji). In between: the prom walkers, the vineyard and two Sea Point prom shots. The Bo-Kaap street, suitcase-on-head and carrying shots, and IMG_1550, were removed on request.
- **The sound:** the "saxophones" sound from the uploaded TikTok. Its sub-bass rumble is cut and the sax tones are boosted so it plays on phone speakers. It's looped, and each pass gets louder (−9 dB → +6 dB).
- **24 s:** record scratch, then "…until you find Cubby" over the app screen recording in a phone on red.
- **Ending:** the suitcase stored at the blue door ("suitcase: stored. holiday: started.") and the end card (Download. Store. Explore., mycubby.co.za).
- The two walkers in IMG_0318 keep their emoji faces.

IMG_1551 was still uploading when this was made. `build.py` adds it automatically once it's in `src/`.

`cubby_saxophones_add_sound_in_tiktok.mp4` is the same edit with only the sound effects (no sax), for adding the trending sound inside TikTok. Line the sound's loud part up with the record scratch at 24 s.
