# Cubby: "The holiday starts here" (10 s)

A cinematic 10-second reel, 1080×1920, text only, built from the existing Cubby B-roll.

| Time | Picture | Text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4.2 s | Slow-motion (0.5×) suitcase-dragging shots: Bo-Kaap, wine farm, the prom. Cool, desaturated grade with film letterbox bars. | "You thought the holiday / *started when you landed?*" | Soft piano and pad |
| 4.2 s | Bars snap away; bright palm-lined Beach Road | **No.** | Music cuts dead, low boom, riser |
| 5.0–5.3 s | The suitcase rolls in and stops at the blue door (Bo-Kaap) | "It starts when you / *drop the suitcase.*" | **Drop** at 5.3 s, with a white flash as the suitcase lands |
| 5.3–8.3 s | Fast warm cuts on the beat: hands-free walk, vineyard, sea-view room, wine farm | (text holds) | Upbeat groove, 120 BPM |
| 8.3–10 s | Cubby end card (logo, Download. Store. Explore., cubby.co.za) | "Welcome to Cubby." | Sparkle and a final chord |

Music is original (`music.py`), so the reel can be posted as is, or muted under a trending sound with its drop near 5 s.
Rebuild: `python build.py` (needs `../src/` B-roll, `../g/endcard.png`, Instrument Serif fonts and the shared SFX).
