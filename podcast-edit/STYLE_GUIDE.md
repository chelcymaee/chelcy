# Podcast reel style guide

Taken from the reference edit (`copy_069B9C34-…` parts 3 then 2). Apply this to every episode.

## Format
- Vertical 9:16, exported at 1080×1920, 30 fps.
- One speaker on screen at a time, with a clean cut when the other person takes over.
- Framing: face fills about one third of the frame width, with eyes and face a little above the middle (around 55% down). The wall above the head stays clear for pop-up graphics.
- When both people talk over each other or react together: a split screen with one person stacked above the other.

## Captions
- Font: **Barlow Condensed ExtraBold**, ALL CAPS, white with a black outline and a soft shadow.
- 1–3 words per caption, on **one line**, breaking at punctuation.
- **The word being spoken turns cyan** (`#31C6E8`), karaoke style.
- Sit at chest height, about 83% down the frame (bottom margin 300 px on a 1920-tall frame).
- Write numbers as digits (150, 32, +59). Correct mis-heard words. Never paraphrase.

## Graphics
- Pop-ups appear **above the speaker's head** in the empty wall space. They drop in quickly, stay for about 2 seconds, then fade out.
- Real images (cards, packs, players) for specific things that are mentioned. Flat cartoon stickers with a white sticker border for general ideas.
- Occasional full-screen effects on hype words (the reference uses fire on "heating up" and falling money on "700K"). Use these sparingly.
- Card images: show the exact card that's mentioned, with readable detail. If it can't be verified, don't show it.

## Pacing and audio
- Trim the sitting-down and standing-up at the start and end.
- Shorten pauses to about 0.2 s. Keep laughs and reactions.
- Audio at −14 LUFS (standard for reels), with a light high-pass, light denoise and gentle compression.
