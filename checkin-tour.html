Balcony

The Balcony

Important — please read carefully

⚠️
Door Stopper — Critical When going out to the balcony, always place the door stopper to hold the door open. If the door slams shut while you are outside, it will lock you out.
🚪
Door Stopper Location The stopper is kept near the balcony door. Slide it under the door or engage it before stepping outside — every single time.

Hot Tub

The Hot Tub

Wood-fired — plan ahead

🕐
Allow 3–4 Hours to Heat The hot tub is heated by fire. Light it 3 to 4 hours before you want to get in so it has time to reach temperature.
1
Remove the lid from the small metal bucket.
2
Place wood inside the metal bucket.
3
Light the fire and replace the lid to keep the heat directed.
4
Stir the water every few minutes when adding more wood — this circulates the heat and makes the whole process significantly faster.
5
Wait 3–4 hours — then enjoy!

The Intercom

Hikvision · opening gates remotely

When someone rings at the gate The Hikvision intercom will alert you. Tap the screen to wake it, then use Live View to see who's there and unlock remotely.
1
Tap the intercom screen to wake it up.
2
Tap Live View (left) — this shows the main gate. Use the unlock button to open it.
3
Tap Live View (right) — this shows the elevator gate. Use the unlock button to open it.
